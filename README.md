# Jazar demo

A phone-friendly proof of concept of Jazar, an offline Arabic–Malay dictionary.

Live: https://jazar-demo.pages.dev/

- Type an Arabic word exactly as you meet it: inflected, with attached particles, with or without vowel marks.
- Results were precomputed by the Jazar search engine for a fixed list of 2,688 school word forms and their headwords; a word outside the list gets a polite note.
- Malay meanings are drafts still under review.

Credits: English meanings from CAMeL-Morph (CC BY 4.0) and English Wiktionary via kaikki.org (CC BY-SA 4.0); some plural forms from English Wiktionary and from Qabas, SinaLab, Birzeit University (CC BY). Fonts: Amiri, Noto Naskh Arabic, Aref Ruqaa, Plus Jakarta Sans (SIL OFL).
